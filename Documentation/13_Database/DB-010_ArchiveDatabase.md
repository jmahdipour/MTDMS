# Purpose

The Archive Database maintains the permanent archive of all engineering documents generated by MTDMS.

The archive stores document references only.

Original TXT files remain the master engineering record.

Archived reports remain read-only.

---

# Objectives

The Archive Database shall

• Archive every released report

• Preserve report history

• Preserve certificate history

• Preserve TXT references

• Support rapid searching

• Support document recovery

---

# Design Philosophy
