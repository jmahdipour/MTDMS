# Purpose

The Report Revision Control Engine manages the complete revision history of every engineering report and laboratory certificate generated by MTDMS.

The engine ensures that previously issued reports remain unchanged while allowing controlled creation of new revisions.

The engine performs **no engineering calculations**.

---

# Objectives

The Report Revision Control Engine shall

• Manage report revisions

• Preserve issued reports

• Track revision history

• Support ISO/IEC 17025 document control

• Maintain engineering traceability
