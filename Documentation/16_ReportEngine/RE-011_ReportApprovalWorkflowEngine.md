# Purpose

The Report Approval Workflow Engine controls the review and approval process for all engineering reports and laboratory certificates generated by MTDMS.

It guarantees that only authorized personnel can approve reports before they are issued.

The engine performs **no engineering calculations**.

---

# Objectives

The Report Approval Workflow Engine shall

• Control report approval

• Support multi-level review

• Prevent unauthorized release

• Record approval history

• Comply with ISO/IEC 17025
