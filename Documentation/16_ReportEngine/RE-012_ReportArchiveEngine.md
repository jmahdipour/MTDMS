# Purpose

The Report Archive Engine provides long-term storage, indexing, retrieval, and protection of all reports and certificates generated by MTDMS.

The archive guarantees that every issued report can be traced back to the original engineering data and ultimately to the original TXT file exported by the testing machine.

The engine performs **no engineering calculations**.

---

# Objectives

The Report Archive Engine shall

• Archive issued reports

• Archive laboratory certificates

• Preserve document history

• Support rapid retrieval

• Maintain complete traceability
