# Purpose

This document defines the internal structure of every Excel workbook generated by MTDMS.

The workbook is the primary engineering report container.

It contains only validated engineering information.

No engineering calculations are performed inside the report workbook.

---

# Design Philosophy

The workbook shall

• Be readable

• Be printable

• Be easy to archive

• Be protected

• Separate engineering data from presentation
