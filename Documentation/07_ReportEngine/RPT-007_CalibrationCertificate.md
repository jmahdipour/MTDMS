# Purpose

This document defines the Calibration Certificate generated by MTDMS.

The certificate documents calibration activities performed on laboratory equipment in accordance with ISO/IEC 17025.

The certificate provides complete measurement traceability, uncertainty reporting, environmental conditions, and approval records.
